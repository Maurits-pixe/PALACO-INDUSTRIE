# BOTERHAM Open Decisions v0.1.0

These decisions remain explicitly open and MUST NOT be silently resolved in implementation:

1. M.C. transferability between users.
2. M.C.7 transferability, expiry and mission-only constraints.
3. First countries/user groups.
4. Initial recognized payment providers.
5. Internal ledger versus blockchain/hybrid boundary.
6. Maximum balances, issuance, transaction and HOTSPOT limits.
7. Authority matrix for coins, rates and HOTSPOT contracts.
8. Final I.E.O. symbol integration.
9. Legal classification and required service permissions.

Until resolved, implementation defaults to SANDBOX/non-financial test assets where applicable.
