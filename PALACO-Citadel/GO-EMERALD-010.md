# ∆ GO-EMERALD-010 — THE EMERALD CONSTITUTION

## Scope

GO-EMERALD-010 establishes the constitutional charter layer for THE EMERALD IMPERIUM above the existing Factory stack.

IMA-CNMNC remains the external mineralogical authority; PALACO governs identification, representation, traceability, and expansion inside the Emerald domain.

## Emerald Imperium Constitution

EIC-001 · v1.0

THE EMERALD IMPERIUM IS A CONSTITUTIONALLY GOVERNED WORLD-DOMAIN FOR MINERAL IDENTITY, PROVENANCE, HISTORY, EXPLORATION AND EXPANSION.

It is not a sovereign state and not a scientific authority above IMA-CNMNC.

## Constitutional articles baseline

1. Identity: each canonical mineral species may map to one unique immutable Emerald World ID.
2. One Mineral, One World: identity is separate from slot, citadel, and elixer layers.
3. 4444 principle: 4444 is current allocation capacity, not full registry cardinality.
4. Expansion: new minerals and new slot capacity are independent events.
5. No truncation: capacity limits never justify identity deletion.
6. Alphabetical initialization: initial ordering by canonical name; identity remains stable over time.
7. Immutable identity: EW IDs never recycled or renumbered.
8. Nomenclature: renames preserve world identity and lineage.
9. Provenance: canonical worlds require source authority, epoch, and source reference.
10. Watermerk: identity/provenance marker, never authority.
11. Hologram: authenticity layer, never truth/authority/sovereignty.
12. Immortal: complete lineage remains reconstructable.
13. Uncertainty: IN_DOUBT and CONFLICTED states are preserved when evidence is insufficient.
14. Z.A.N.D.: determine first; never infer canonical identity from guesses.
15. Relationships: typed relation edges with evidence.
16. Citadel: bounded context and interaction layer, not mineral identity itself.
17. Elixers: exploration/verification tools without constitutional authority.
18. Allocation: OW slot assignment is mutable; EW identity is not.
19. ∆ transitions: state changes require context, evidence, decision, authorization, and traceability.
20. Revoke: revokes allocation state, never deletes identity.
21. CEFCG: finality model states remain explicitly separated.
22. No mineral sovereignty: no world object gains political or constitutional authority.
23. PALACO supremacy: PALACO Constitution outranks all Emerald constitution/governance/policy/action layers.
24. Expansion horizon: architectural capacity is open-ended under constitutional control.
25. Living Emerald: present/history/future layers evolve without outranking constitution.
