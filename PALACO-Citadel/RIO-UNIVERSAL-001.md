# RIO-UNIVERSAL-001 — UNIVERSAL CONVERSATION LAYER

## Canonical direction

ONE RIO. MANY SURFACES. ONE CONVERSATIONAL CONTEXT.

RIO is a universal conversational entry point to PALACO across mobile, web, ELIXER, Citadel, and future supported environments.

## Universal components

- RIO Identity: who/what communicates.
- RIO Address: human-readable destination resolved to valid endpoint.
- RIO Link: portable entry to communication destination.
- RIO Handshake: start flow with identity/context/access policy checks.
- RIO Trust Indicators: verified, insufficient verification, unverified, unknown.
- RIO Conversation State: discovered, requested, connected, active, paused, resumed, closed (+ disputed/conflicted/in-doubt states).

## Governance bindings

- Communication is not finality; CEFCG governs event status where relevant.
- Revoke withdraws authorization and does not erase communication history.
- Privacy visibility remains user-facing: who can see what, why, how long, and with whom shared.
- TRIAS remains the final constitutional boundary for constitutionally relevant actions.
