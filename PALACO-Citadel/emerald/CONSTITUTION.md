# EMERALD IMPERIUM CONSTITUTION

EIC-001 · v1.0

THE EMERALD IMPERIUM IS A CONSTITUTIONALLY GOVERNED WORLD-DOMAIN FOR MINERAL IDENTITY, PROVENANCE, HISTORY, EXPLORATION AND EXPANSION.

The Emerald Imperium is not a sovereign authority and does not supersede IMA-CNMNC mineralogical authority.

## ARTICLE I — IDENTITY

Each canonical mineral species may be represented by one unique immutable Emerald World ID linked to lineage.

## ARTICLE II — ONE MINERAL, ONE WORLD

ONE CANONICAL MINERAL IDENTITY → ONE EMERALD WORLD ID.

MINERAL ≠ WORLD SLOT. WORLD ≠ CITADEL. WORLD ≠ ELIXER.

## ARTICLE III — THE 4444 PRINCIPLE

4444 OTHER WORLDS is current allocation capacity, not full registry size.

Registry records above 4444 must be preserved as expansion-ready, never deleted.

## ARTICLE IV — EXPANSION

New minerals and new slot capacity are independent constitutional events.

## ARTICLE V — NO TRUNCATION

Capacity limitation shall never become identity deletion.

## ARTICLE VI — ALPHABETICAL ORDER

Initial canonical sequence uses alphabetical ordering by canonical mineral name.

Ordering never overrides established immutable identity.

## ARTICLE VII — IMMUTABLE IDENTITY

EW IDs are permanent and never recycled, including rename/redefine/slot-change/revoke/expansion events.

## ARTICLE VIII — NOMENCLATURE

Name transitions preserve the same EW identity and retain aliases, historical names, and lineage.

## ARTICLE IX — PROVENANCE

Canonical worlds require provenance with source authority, source epoch, and source reference.

No provenance → no canonical world.

## ARTICLE X — WATERMERK

Each Emerald World receives EM-WM-EW-XXXX identity/provenance marker.

WATERMERK ≠ AUTHORITY.

## ARTICLE XI — HOLOGRAM

Each interactive world receives EM-HO-EW-XXXX authenticity layer.

HOLOGRAM ≠ TRUTH. HOLOGRAM ≠ AUTHORITY. HOLOGRAM ≠ SOVEREIGNTY.

## ARTICLE XII — IMMORTAL

Historical states are never silently removed; full lineage remains reconstructable.

## ARTICLE XIII — UNCERTAINTY

IN_DOUBT and CONFLICTED states are valid for uncertain evidence; uncertainty may not be laundered into certainty.

## ARTICLE XIV — Z.A.N.D.

Determine first, never infer canonical identity from AI guesses.

## ARTICLE XV — RELATIONSHIPS

Relationships are typed (structural, chemical, group, paragenetic, historical) and evidence-bound.

## ARTICLE XVI — CITADEL

Citadel provides bounded context, interaction, and evidence presentation; it is not the mineral.

## ARTICLE XVII — ELIXERS

ELIXERS may search/identify/explore/compare/visualize/trace/verify, but may not authorize/govern/command/claim sovereignty.

## ARTICLE XVIII — ALLOCATION

World slot is separate mutable resource:

EW-XXXX → OW-XXXX

Allocation changes; world identity does not.

## ARTICLE XIX — ∆

Meaningful state transitions use controlled ∆ with required context, evidence, decision, authorization, and traceability.

## ARTICLE XX — REVOKE

REVOKE may return ACTIVE to PENDING/UNALLOCATED without deleting EW identity.

## ARTICLE XXI — CEFCG

Evidence progression may use OBSERVED/ATTESTED/PROVEN/CONFIRMED/FINAL plus DISPUTED/CONFLICTED/IN_DOUBT.

PROVEN ≠ FINAL. CONFIRMED ≠ IRREVERSIBLE. FINAL ≠ SUCCESS.

## ARTICLE XXII — NO MINERAL SOVEREIGNTY

No mineral-world object grants political, constitutional, sovereignty, or command authority.

## ARTICLE XXIII — PALACO SUPREMACY

PALACO CONSTITUTION > EMERALD CONSTITUTION > EMERALD GOVERNANCE > EMERALD POLICY > EMERALD ACTION.

Any Emerald rule conflicting with PALACO Constitution is invalid.

## ARTICLE XXIV — EXPANSION HORIZON

Expansion horizon is open-ended architectural capability under constitutional control (4444 → 5444 → 6239 → 7000 → 10000 → ...).

## ARTICLE XXV — THE LIVING EMERALD

Emerald Imperium is a living mineral registry across present, history, and future expansion layers.

Evolution is allowed, but EVOLUTION SHALL NOT OUTRANK CONSTITUTION.

## CANONICAL SENTENCE

THE EMERALD IMPERIUM SHALL NEVER BE LIMITED BY THE NUMBER OF WORLDS IT CURRENTLY HAS THE CAPACITY TO DISPLAY.
