# RIO-FABRIC-001 — CONTINUOUS COMMUNICATION

## Canonical law

RIO SHALL PROVIDE CONTINUOUS, CONTEXT-AWARE COMMUNICATION ACROSS SUPPORTED PALACO SURFACES, INCLUDING MOBILE, WEB, CITADEL AND ELIXER ENVIRONMENTS, WITHOUT MAKING COMMUNICATION, PRESENCE, MEMBERSHIP OR CONNECTIVITY EQUIVALENT TO AUTHORITY OR AUTHORIZATION.

## Core formula

RIO CONNECTS.
VORM9EVIN9 EXPLAINS.
CONSTITUTION GOVERNS.

## Fabric capabilities

- RIO Rooms: conversation contexts with identity, participants, context, access, provenance, state, and history.
- RIO Bridge: controlled linking of communication contexts across surfaces.
- RIO Handoff: continuity between old and new surface after identity/context/access checks.
- Cross-surface continuity: same conversation across mobile, web, and ELIXER contexts.

## Delivery semantics

Message lifecycle may include CREATED, QUEUED, DELIVERED, RECEIVED, READ, ACKNOWLEDGED.

## Hard constraints

- Joining a room does not grant constitutional authority.
- Presence does not grant authority.
- Communication does not equal authorization.
- Machine communication does not create constitutional power.
